Hi! I am an integrated M.S.–Ph.D. student in the 3D Vision & Robotics Lab at UNIST, advised by Kyungdon Joo.

I am interested in SLAM, 3D reconstruction, and collaborative robot perception. My research connects geometric foundation models with consistent online mapping, neural scene representations, and learning across heterogeneous robots.

I also enjoy building robotic systems—from autonomous driving and embedded control to interactive computer vision.

Cooperative Driving at Intersections

2020 · Undergraduate project

Combined vehicle-to-infrastructure (V2I) communication with camera-based vehicle localization for intersection collision avoidance. Used ArUco markers to estimate vehicle positions relative to the traffic-light camera and tested scenarios with a red-light-violating vehicle.

ROS · V2I communication · Computer vision · ArUco localization · Collision avoidance

Indoor Delivery Robot

Integrated GMapping for map creation, AMCL for localization, and the Dynamic Window Approach (DWA) for local navigation in an indoor delivery robot. Added QR-code-based recipient verification at the delivery destination.

RANSAC-based Lane Detection

2019 · 3rd-year undergraduate

A ROS vision pipeline that transforms camera images into a bird’s-eye view, parameterizes lane lines in cylindrical coordinates, and fits white and yellow lanes with sliding-window RANSAC to generate waypoints for lane following.

C++ · ROS · Computer vision · RANSAC
